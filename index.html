THE QUESTION CHANGES

We had tried to show the artwork.
We had never helped the artists meet.

Grace saw something different in Ezekiel’s work: not an image to promote, but a person she wanted to create with. That shift—from artwork to artist—opened a direction Voice of Piece had never imagined.

THE THIRD WORK

Neither one erased.
Something else born between them.

Ezekiel’s lion carries a sense of home and identity. Grace’s faces explore how identity changes through time and experience. The shared composition brings these ideas together while preserving the meaning of each original work.

THE ORIGIN OF ARTBRIDGE

The meeting revealed new questions: How do artists find one another? How can meaning cross languages? How can a shared work protect both names, both voices and both futures? ArtBridge grew from those questions.

ONE PLACE, FROM INTRODUCTION TO AGREEMENT

A meeting becomes
a way forward.

ArtBridge is envisioned as one continuous space where artists can be seen, discover a reciprocal connection, shape a project and write the terms that protect their collaboration.

  1. 01

    Artist profile

    Share your practice, themes, language and what you hope to find.

  2. 02

    Meaningful matching

    See reciprocal possibilities and understand why a connection is suggested.

  3. 03

    Shared project brief

    Turn an initial spark into a purpose, contribution and timeline both artists can shape.

  4. 04

    Terms & agreement

    Draft credit, permissions, revenue and boundaries before the work travels.

  5. 05

    Translation & review

    Keep every original while reviewing English, Korean and Kiswahili together.

  6. 06

    Creation archive

    Hold the evolving brief, notes and decisions as part of the work’s history.
