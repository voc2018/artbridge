IN HER OWN WORDS

Chronomorphosis,
in Grace’s own words.

Grace LeeLOS ANGELES, USAWritten in English

The meaning behind my artwork comes from a piece I originally created called Chronomorphosis, which explores how my identity changes over time. I used the shape of the United States as the foundation, with different faces emerging from the map to represent the different versions of myself shaped by time, experience, and the people around me. As these faces overlap and blend together, it becomes difficult to distinguish who I am now from who I have been and how others perceive me. To me, identity is not fixed; it is constantly evolving as we move through different stages of life.

When I connected my work with your lion and Africa, I saw a similar idea. Your lion represents a part of your home and identity, while the face in my artwork represents myself and my connection to America. Bringing our works together allowed us to share parts of who we are and where we come from through art.

What makes this collaboration especially meaningful to me is that we created something new without changing or taking away from the original meaning of either artwork. Although we come from different countries and backgrounds, our works could exist alongside each other and form something that belonged to both of us.

The Question That Didn’t Exist Before We Met

Chuyeon Lee · President, Voice of Calling

1The Lion and the Bison

A lion and a bison will never cross paths in their entire lives.

One lives on the savannas of Africa, the other on the plains of North America. Between them lies an ocean.

But this year, I saw the two of them recognize each other.

Ezekiel, a young painter from Tanzania, painted a lion inside the outline of the African continent. Grace, a high school student in Los Angeles, painted a bison inside the outline of the American continent.

The two had never met. They didn't even speak the same language.

But when I placed the two paintings side by side, I held my breath for a moment.

Because it looked as if they had been searching for each other for a long time.

I want to tell you what happened when these two paintings met. And about what that meeting taught me: the true meaning of the word we hear most often in the world these days, fusion.

2We Only Tried to Show

In Tanzania, there are painters who paint all day long, yet still struggle to make a living from their paintings.

It is not because they lack talent. It is because no one knows who they are.

We met those painters, and we started Voice of Piece.

We were people who were good at technology. So we gave our hearts in the way we knew how. We built websites, put their works online, and wrote their stories in many languages. We made merchandise and worked hard to promote them. We also sold some of the painters' works in the United States and returned the proceeds to them.

We believed we were doing good work. And that was true.

But looking back now, through all that time, we were holding on to just one question.

"How can we show this painting to more people?"

Showing. That was where we poured all our effort.

3The Question Changes

Then, in our third cohort, Grace joined us as a leader.

Grace is not someone who writes code. She is someone who paints.

It reminded me of how, before Steve Jobs made the Macintosh at Apple, he sat in on a calligraphy class that seemed far removed from engineering.

Grace looked at Ezekiel's painting with completely different eyes from our engineers.

In that painting, our engineers saw "a work that needed to be promoted." In that painting, Grace saw "a person she wanted to paint with."

That small difference changed everything.

Between the two of them, a painting that had never existed in the world was born.

That day, the road Voice of Piece had been walking since 2024 opened in a completely different direction.

Standing before that painting, I realized.

That all this time, we had only tried to show them to the world.

That not once had we ever let them meet someone.

That day, our question changed.

"If artists who could never have met were to meet, what could be born?"

Our eyes had moved from the artwork to the artist, from the painting to the person.

This question would never have come out of a room full of engineers.

The moment an artist's eyes came in, we began to see a world we had not been able to see.

4Writing Names Side by Side

But the beautiful moment did not end there. The real difficulty came after.

The languages were different. The cultures were different. For one person's thoughts to reach the other, they had to pass through many translations and many misunderstandings.

And questions we had never imagined came pouring in.

  • Whose name goes next to this painting, and how do we write the names side by side?
  • What do we do about copyright?
  • If the painting sells, how do we share the proceeds?
  • How far can the original work be transformed?
  • Who bears the cost of promotion, and how?

At first, I thought this was a paperwork problem. It wasn't.

It was a matter of respect.

Would the name of a young painter across the ocean, his share, his rights, be properly protected? If we could not answer that question, even the most beautiful collaboration could become just another unfair story.

That was when the engineers came back to the table.

But this time was different. In the beginning, we had defined the problem and built the technology. This time, the engineers took up, with both hands, the wall that an artist had actually run into.

And they began to ask.

  • Could we help a painter in LA and a painter in Tanzania discover that the other exists?
  • Even if their languages differ, could we help their hearts come across as they truly are?
  • When two people decide to join hands, could we help them write, together, the promises that will protect each other?

From those questions, ArtBridge was born.

ArtBridge does not paint for anyone. It does not automate the artist either.

It is a bridge that lets two people who would never have met discover each other.

Art gave technology a question, and technology turned that question into a path that reaches more people.

Without Grace, there would be no ArtBridge.

Without the engineers, that meeting would have ended as one beautiful coincidence.

5The People Who Didn't Have the Words "That's Not My Field"

Do you know who made all of this?

Not experts at research institutes, nor researchers at prestigious universities.

High school students.

One student painted. One student wrote code. One student is preparing to go find the painters of LA in person and listen to their stories. One student wrestled with copyright and contracts.

No one planned this from the beginning. There was a real problem in front of them, and that problem simply made them need one another.

These students did not yet have these words.

"That's not my field."

Adults, myself included, say these words far too often. Because I'm an engineer. Because I'm a designer. Because I'm in business. We neither take an interest in other people's fields nor try them. At some point, we start to think that staying inside the area we already know well is more comfortable and safer.

In chemistry, an atom bonds with other atoms when its outer shell has empty spaces.

Hydrogen is a gas that explodes when it catches fire, and oxygen is a gas that makes fire burn bigger. But when two hydrogens fill oxygen's two empty spaces and bond, they become water. Water that puts out fire. Something that resembles neither of them, something that is, in a way, their very opposite, has been newly created.

On the other hand, an element like neon, whose shell is already full, bonds with no one. Born as neon, it lives as neon all the way.

The students are not neon yet. They still have many empty spaces, and still many questions.

And I now believe that this incompleteness is not a lack, but a place where something new can be born.

To be honest, ArtBridge is not finished yet. It has just taken its first step, and the students are still researching and refining it.

But there is something that strikes me even more deeply.

ArtBridge did not come out of someone's grand plan. It was born from the effort of students with different interests, each simply in the place they were given, with what they had, sincerely striving to make the best thing they could.

To me, that fact means more than any finished result.

6The Question That Didn't Exist Before We Met

Let's go back to the lion and the bison.

A lion and a bison will never cross paths in their lives. But at the tips of two people's brushes, they recognized each other.

And that meeting did not end with a single painting. Grace, the engineers, and we are no longer the same since that meeting.

Now I think I can tell you what that meeting taught me. What is the true meaning of fusion?

Is it using AI to turn my portrait into a Van Gogh-style painting, or to turn a family photo into Ghibli style?

These days, every project and every piece of research, even college admissions, asks this: "What social problem do you want to solve? What solution will you offer for that problem?"

So we have ended up having to create problems first, just to offer solutions. Because we are told we must fuse the humanities and the sciences, that art and technology must be connected, we even force those connections.

But Grace and Ezekiel's story began the opposite way.

No one said from the start, "Let's make a fusion project."

No one said, "Let's build a solution called ArtBridge."

First there were people, and there was a real meeting.

Within that meeting, difficulties we had not expected arose. A real problem appeared in front of us.

And when we began to look at that problem properly, the technology we needed followed.

Now I can say this.

True fusion is the birth of something new that neither of the two could ever have made alone.

Like a third painting that is neither Ezekiel's nor Grace's. ArtBridge was born that way too.

That new thing always begins with a question that did not exist before the meeting. And because of that question, everyone who I met became a slightly different person than before.

So fusion cannot be forced. What we can do is create a place where different worlds can truly meet. That is where fusion begins. Because some possibilities remain invisible until you meet another world.

That is also why I do not want to measure ArtBridge by the number of registered artists or followers. Two people who would never have met without ArtBridge meeting each other, and one more painting that would never have existed coming into the world. That is why ArtBridge exists.

2우리는 보여주려고만 했습니다

탄자니아에는 하루 종일 그림을 그리고도, 그 그림으로 살아가기 어려운 화가들이 있습니다.

우리는 기술을 잘하는 사람들이었습니다. 그래서 우리가 아는 방식으로 마음을 다했습니다. 웹사이트를 만들고, 작품을 온라인에 올리고, 여러 언어로 그들의 이야기를 썼습니다. 굿즈를 만들어서 그들을 홍보하려고 애를 썼습니다. 몇몇 화가의 작품을 미국에서 판매하여 그 수익을 화가들에게 돌려주기도 했습니다.

우리는 좋은 일을 하고 있다고 믿었습니다. 그리고 그건 사실이었습니다.

그런데 지금 돌아보면, 우리는 그 모든 시간 동안 단 하나의 질문만 붙들고 있었습니다.

"이 그림을, 어떻게 하면 더 많은 사람에게 보여줄 수 있을까?"

보여주는 것. 그것이 우리가 온 힘을 쏟은 일이었습니다.

3질문이 바뀌다

그러다 세 번째 기수에 Grace가 리더로 합류했습니다.

Grace는 코드를 쓰지 않는 사람입니다. 그림을 그리는 사람입니다.

그때 저는 스티브 잡스가 애플에서 매킨토시를 만들기 전, 엔지니어링과는 거리가 멀어 보이는 캘리그래피 수업을 청강했던 일이 떠올랐습니다.

Grace는 Ezekiel의 그림을 우리 엔지니어들과 전혀 다른 눈으로 봤습니다.

엔지니어들은 그 그림에서 '알려야 할 작품'을 봤습니다. Grace는 그 그림에서 '함께 그리고 싶은 사람'을 봤습니다.

그 작은 차이가, 모든 것을 바꿨습니다.

두 사람 사이에서 세상에 없던 그림이 태어났습니다.

2024년부터 걸어온 Voice of Piece의 길이, 그날 전혀 다른 방향으로 열렸습니다.

그 그림 앞에서 저는 깨달았습니다.

우리는 그동안 그들을 세상에 보여주려고만 했다는 것을.

한 번도, 그들을 누군가와 만나게 해준 적은 없었다는 것을.

그날 우리의 질문이 바뀌었습니다.

"서로 만날 수 없었던 예술가들이 만난다면, 무엇이 태어날 수 있을까?"

우리의 시선이 작품에서 예술가에게로, 그림에서 사람에게로 옮겨간 겁니다.

이 질문은 엔지니어들만 모인 방에서는 절대 나오지 않았을 겁니다.

예술가의 눈이 들어온 순간, 우리가 보지 못하던 세계가 보이기 시작했습니다.

4이름을 나란히 적는다는 것

하지만 아름다운 순간은 거기서 끝나지 않았습니다. 진짜 어려움은 그다음부터였습니다.

언어가 달랐습니다. 문화가 달랐습니다. 한 사람의 생각이 다른 사람에게 닿기까지, 몇 번의 번역과 몇 번의 오해를 지나야 했습니다.

그리고 생각지도 못한 질문들이 쏟아졌습니다.

  • 이 그림 옆에 누구의 이름을, 어떻게 나란히 적을 것인가.
  • 저작권은 어떻게 할 것인가.
  • 그림이 팔리면, 그 수익은 어떻게 나눌 것인가.
  • 원작을 어디까지 변형해도 되는가.
  • 홍보를 위한 비용은 누가 어떻게 감당할 것인가.

처음엔 이게 서류 문제인 줄 알았습니다. 아니었습니다.

바로 그때, 엔지니어들이 다시 테이블로 돌아왔습니다.

하지만 이번엔 달랐습니다. 처음엔 우리가 문제를 정하고 기술을 만들었습니다. 이번엔 한 예술가가 직접 부딪힌 벽을, 엔지니어들이 두 손으로 받아 들었습니다.

그리고 묻기 시작했습니다.

  • LA의 화가와 탄자니아의 화가가 서로의 존재를 알게 할 수 있을까?
  • 말이 달라도, 마음은 제대로 전해지게 할 수 있을까?
  • 두 사람이 손을 잡기로 했을 때, 서로를 지켜줄 약속을 함께 쓰도록 도울 수 있을까?

그 질문들에서 ArtBridge가 태어났습니다.

ArtBridge는 그림을 대신 그리지 않습니다. 예술가를 자동화하지도 않습니다.

만나지 못했을 두 사람이 서로를 발견하게 하는, 다리입니다.

예술이 기술에게 질문을 주었고, 기술은 그 질문을 더 많은 사람에게 닿는 길로 바꾸었습니다.

Grace가 없었다면, ArtBridge는 없었습니다.

엔지니어들이 없었다면, 그 만남은 한 번의 아름다운 우연으로 끝났을 겁니다.

Research · How ArtBridge suggests a meeting

A meeting both artists would choose

Most recommenders ask one question: what will this person like? ArtBridge has to ask two at once — would you want to create with them, and would they want to create with you? This page shows exactly how we compute that, which research it rests on, and what we have not proven yet.

1The problem is reciprocal

On a shopping site, a book cannot refuse you. On ArtBridge, every “item” is a person with their own wishes. A suggestion that delights one artist and leaves the other cold is not a meeting — it is a polite rejection waiting to happen.

Research calls this reciprocal recommendation: people recommended to people, where success requires interest on both sides (Pizzato et al., 2010; Palomares et al., 2021). ArtBridge is built on that idea. Every number below is computed twice — once from each artist’s point of view — and then combined so that neither side can be ignored.

Everything is computed only from what artists themselves chose in the application: the same eight matching questions, with the same answer options, for artists in Tanzania and in Los Angeles.

2Four rules before any score

A pair receives no score at all — it is simply never shown — unless all four rules hold:

  1. Both artists agreed to be suggested. “Not yet” means invisible.
  2. Each is in the region the other is looking for. (For LA applicants, the partner region is Tanzania.)
  3. Both answered the four matching lists — what I bring, what I seek, my themes, how I want to work. Missing answers never produce a confident guess.
  4. They share at least one way of working (e.g. both chose “joint artwork”). Two artists who want entirely different kinds of projects are not a meeting.

3Three measures, with exact weights

For a pair that passes, we measure three things. Each is a number between 0 and 1.

Shared themes — Jaccard similarity weight 0.35

Jtheme = |TA ∩ TB| ÷ |TA ∪ TB|

Themes both chose, divided by all themes either chose. Dividing by the union means ticking every box does not inflate a match (Jaccard, 1912).

Complementary techniques — coverage weight 0.40

CA→B = |SeekA ∩ BringB| ÷ |SeekA|

Of the techniques A hopes to find, the share B can bring. This one is directional: B may cover all of A’s wishes while A covers only half of B’s. That asymmetry is exactly what reciprocal scoring must see.

Ways of working — Jaccard similarity weight 0.25

Jformat = |FA ∩ FB| ÷ |FA ∪ FB|

Joint artwork, exhibition, products, letter and sketch exchange, workshop, video.

The weights add to 1, so each directed score is also between 0 and 1:

SA→B = 0.35·Jtheme + 0.40·CA→B + 0.25·Jformat

SB→A = 0.35·Jtheme + 0.40·CB→A + 0.25·Jformat

4Combining two directions: the harmonic mean

We now have two numbers — how well B fits A, and how well A fits B. We combine them with the harmonic mean and show it out of 100:

Connection = round( 100 × 2·SA→B·SB→A ÷ (SA→B + SB→A) )

  1. Rules: both consented, regions match, all lists answered, shared format (Joint artwork). ✓
  2. Themes: shared {Wildlife, Family & love} = 2; all = 5 → Jtheme = 2/5 = 0.400
  3. Formats: shared 1; all 3 → Jformat = 1/3 = 0.333
  4. A’s wishes: B brings both Tingatinga and Painting → CA→B = 2/2 = 1.000
  5. B’s wishes: A brings Digital art but not Photography → CB→A = 1/2 = 0.500
  6. SA→B = 0.35×0.400 + 0.40×1.000 + 0.25×0.333 = 0.140 + 0.400 + 0.083 = 0.623
  7. SB→A = 0.140 + 0.40×0.500 + 0.083 = 0.423
  8. Harmonic mean = 2×0.623×0.423 ÷ (0.623+0.423) = 0.504 → Connection 50 / 100 (an arithmetic mean would have said 52)
  9. Agreed transformation: the more protective answer wins → “Combined, not redrawn.” This is not scored; it is carried into the promise both artists write.

The artists see the reasons, not just the number: shared themes, what each brings the other, and the shared format. Dates and schedules are worked out together after the artists agree to meet.

6Why these choices

Why complementarity weighs most (0.40). In a controlled experiment, Büyükboyacı and Robbett (2019) found a strong positive interaction between letting people choose their own teams and letting them specialise: self-chosen teams did best when members’ skills complemented each other. ArtBridge follows both halves — it rewards what each artist can bring the other, and it never assigns partners; artists choose.

Why themes still matter (0.35). Complementary hands need a shared subject to work on. Lion and bison, mother and child, hope: a common theme gives two strangers something to talk about before they share a language.

Why formats are both a rule and a weight (0.25). With no shared format there is no project, so it is a rule. Beyond one shared format, more agreement makes planning easier, so it also counts.

Why harmonic mean. Mutual interest is the objective of reciprocal recommendation (Vitale et al., 2018; Palomares, 2020). The harmonic mean encodes that objective directly, and it is the operator the literature has tested most (Palomares et al., 2021).

What the papers do not give us: the exact weights 0.35 / 0.40 / 0.25. Those are our design choices, written down so they can be tested and changed — see section 9.

7What we deliberately refuse to use

ApproachWhy it is temptingWhy not now
Popularity, followers, salesEasy to measureShows the already-visible again. ArtBridge exists for artists no one knows yet.
Judging the artwork with AIFeels “smart”ArtBridge does not rank art. It does not paint for anyone, and it does not judge anyone’s painting.
Collaborative filteringLearns from behaviourA small first cohort has no reliable history; it would learn noise.
Language-model embeddingsReads free text in any languageOpaque, costly, and unvalidated for this use. Artists’ own choices come first.
Automatic one-to-one assignmentOptimises a whole cohortTreats a score as consent. Every meeting must be chosen by both artists.

Language never lowers a score. Kiswahili, English and Korean are handled by translation, with the original always kept beside it.

8Safeguards

  • A suggestion is not a match. Saving someone or drafting a project sends nothing. Artists meet only when both say yes, and either can say no at any time.
  • The more protective answer wins on how far a work may be transformed.
  • Artists under 18 need a parent or guardian’s name, email and consent before they can be matched.
  • Data stays small. Only the answers in the application are used. Before the database exists, applications are stored in a private local CSV file.

9What we have not proven yet

We will not claim ArtBridge “works” until the artists tell us. The pilot will compare our score with two simpler baselines — shared themes only and random eligible pairs — and measure:

MeasureDefinition
Mutual interest rateSuggestions where both artists say yes ÷ suggestions shown
Explanation usefulnessArtists rate whether the reasons shown made sense (1–5)
Decline reasonsWhy an artist said no — to find what the questions miss
Completed worksMeetings that produce a finished shared work with signed promises
Fair exposureHow evenly suggestions are spread across artists, so the same few are not shown again and again
Weight sensitivityHow rankings change if each weight moves ±0.10

As the essay that started this project says, we do not measure ArtBridge by registered artists or followers. We measure it by two people who would never have met, meeting — and one more painting that would never have existed.

리서치 · ArtBridge는 만남을 어떻게 제안하나

두 작가 모두가 고를 만남

대부분의 추천 시스템은 한 가지만 묻습니다. 이 사람은 무엇을 좋아할까? ArtBridge는 두 가지를 동시에 물어야 합니다. 나는 이 사람과 함께 작업하고 싶은가, 그리고 그 사람도 나와 함께 작업하고 싶은가? 이 페이지는 그것을 정확히 어떻게 계산하는지, 어떤 연구에 기대고 있는지, 그리고 아직 무엇을 증명하지 못했는지 보여줍니다.

0인기, 판매, 팔로워, 작품의 "수준"에 관한 신호

1문제는 양방향입니다

쇼핑 사이트에서 책은 당신을 거절할 수 없습니다. ArtBridge에서는 모든 "추천 대상"이 자기만의 바람을 가진 사람입니다. 한 작가는 기뻐하고 다른 작가는 시큰둥한 제안은 만남이 아닙니다. 예의 바른 거절이 기다리고 있을 뿐입니다.

연구에서는 이것을 상호 추천(reciprocal recommendation)이라고 부릅니다. 사람에게 사람을 추천하는 것으로, 양쪽 모두 관심이 있어야 성공입니다(Pizzato et al., 2010; Palomares et al., 2021). ArtBridge는 이 생각 위에 세워졌습니다. 아래의 모든 숫자는 두 번 계산됩니다. 각 작가의 입장에서 한 번씩 계산한 뒤, 어느 쪽도 무시되지 않도록 합칩니다.

모든 계산은 작가가 신청서에서 직접 고른 답만으로 이루어집니다. 탄자니아와 로스앤젤레스의 작가가 같은 여덟 개의 매칭 질문에 같은 선택지로 답합니다.

2점수를 매기기 전의 네 가지 규칙

네 가지 규칙을 모두 만족하지 않으면 그 짝은 점수를 받지 않고, 아예 보이지 않습니다.

  1. 두 작가 모두 추천되는 것에 동의했습니다. "아직은 아니요"는 보이지 않는다는 뜻입니다.
  2. 각자 상대가 찾는 지역에 있습니다. (LA 신청자의 상대 지역은 탄자니아입니다.)
  3. 두 사람 모두 네 가지 매칭 목록에 답했습니다 — 내가 가져오는 것, 내가 찾는 것, 나의 주제, 원하는 작업 방식. 빠진 답으로 확신에 찬 추측을 만들지 않습니다.
  4. 적어도 하나의 작업 방식이 같습니다 (예: 둘 다 "공동 작품"을 선택). 전혀 다른 종류의 프로젝트를 원하는 두 작가는 만남이 될 수 없습니다.

3세 가지 측정값과 정확한 가중치

규칙을 통과한 짝에 대해 세 가지를 잽니다. 각각 0과 1 사이의 숫자입니다.

공통 주제 — 자카드 유사도 가중치 0.35

Jtheme = |TA ∩ TB| ÷ |TA ∪ TB|

두 사람이 모두 고른 주제를, 한 사람이라도 고른 주제 전체로 나눈 값입니다. 합집합으로 나누기 때문에 모든 칸에 체크해도 점수가 부풀지 않습니다(Jaccard, 1912).

서로 채워주는 기법 — 충족률 가중치 0.40

CA→B = |SeekA ∩ BringB| ÷ |SeekA|

A가 찾는 기법 가운데 B가 가져올 수 있는 비율입니다. 이 값은 방향이 있습니다. B는 A의 바람을 모두 채워도, A는 B의 바람을 절반만 채울 수 있습니다. 상호 점수가 바로 이 비대칭을 보아야 합니다.

작업 방식 — 자카드 유사도 가중치 0.25

Jformat = |FA ∩ FB| ÷ |FA ∪ FB|

공동 작품, 전시, 상품, 편지와 스케치 교환, 워크숍, 영상.

가중치의 합은 1이므로, 방향별 점수도 0과 1 사이입니다.

SA→B = 0.35·Jtheme + 0.40·CA→B + 0.25·Jformat

SB→A = 0.35·Jtheme + 0.40·CB→A + 0.25·Jformat

4두 방향 합치기: 조화평균

이제 숫자가 두 개 있습니다. B가 A에게 얼마나 맞는지, A가 B에게 얼마나 맞는지. 이 둘을 조화평균으로 합쳐 100점 만점으로 보여줍니다.

연결 점수 = round( 100 × 2·SA→B·SB→A ÷ (SA→B + SB→A) )

조화평균은 두 숫자 가운데 작은 쪽에 가깝게 머뭅니다. 한쪽만 맞는 짝은 평균 뒤에 숨을 수 없습니다.

5계산 예시

가상의 두 작가를, 사이트에서 실제로 돌아가는 같은 코드로 계산했습니다.

작가 A · 로스앤젤레스작가 B · 탄자니아
내가 가져오는 것드로잉, 디지털 아트팅가팅가, 회화
내가 찾는 것팅가팅가, 회화디지털 아트, 사진
주제야생동물, 가족과 사랑, 정체성야생동물, 가족과 사랑, 문화, 공동체
작업 방식공동 작품, 전시공동 작품, 아트 상품
내 작품은…합쳐도 되지만 다시 그리면 안 됨일부를 다시 그려도 됨
  1. 규칙: 둘 다 동의, 지역 일치, 모든 목록 답변, 공통 작업 방식(공동 작품). ✓
  2. 주제: 공통 {야생동물, 가족과 사랑} = 2개, 전체 = 5개 → Jtheme = 2/5 = 0.400
  3. 작업 방식: 공통 1개, 전체 3개 → Jformat = 1/3 = 0.333
  4. A의 바람: B가 팅가팅가와 회화를 모두 가져옴 → CA→B = 2/2 = 1.000
  5. B의 바람: A가 디지털 아트는 가져오지만 사진은 아님 → CB→A = 1/2 = 0.500
  6. SA→B = 0.35×0.400 + 0.40×1.000 + 0.25×0.333 = 0.140 + 0.400 + 0.083 = 0.623
  7. SB→A = 0.140 + 0.40×0.500 + 0.083 = 0.423
  8. 조화평균 = 2×0.623×0.423 ÷ (0.623+0.423) = 0.504 → 연결 점수 50 / 100 (산술평균이었다면 52)
  9. 합의된 변형 범위: 더 보호적인 답이 우선합니다 → "합쳐도 되지만 다시 그리면 안 됨." 이것은 점수에 들어가지 않고, 두 작가가 함께 쓰는 약속으로 넘어갑니다.

작가들은 숫자만이 아니라 이유를 봅니다. 공통 주제, 서로에게 가져올 수 있는 것, 공통 작업 방식. 일정은 두 작가가 만나기로 한 뒤에 함께 정합니다.

6왜 이렇게 정했나

왜 상호 보완이 가장 무거운가 (0.40). 통제 실험에서 Büyükboyacı와 Robbett(2019)는 사람들이 팀을 스스로 고르게 하는 것과 전문화하게 하는 것 사이에 강한 긍정적 상호작용이 있음을 발견했습니다. 스스로 고른 팀은 구성원의 기술이 서로를 보완할 때 가장 좋은 성과를 냈습니다. ArtBridge는 두 가지를 모두 따릅니다. 각 작가가 상대에게 가져올 수 있는 것에 점수를 주고, 짝을 배정하지 않습니다. 작가가 고릅니다.

왜 주제도 여전히 중요한가 (0.35). 서로 채워주는 손에도 함께 다룰 소재가 필요합니다. 사자와 들소, 엄마와 아이, 희망. 공통 주제는 말이 통하기 전의 두 낯선 사람에게 이야깃거리를 줍니다.

왜 작업 방식은 규칙이면서 가중치인가 (0.25). 공통 작업 방식이 없으면 프로젝트도 없으므로 규칙입니다. 하나를 넘어 더 많이 겹칠수록 계획이 쉬워지므로 점수에도 반영합니다.

왜 조화평균인가. 상호 관심은 상호 추천의 목표입니다(Vitale et al., 2018; Palomares, 2020). 조화평균은 그 목표를 그대로 담고 있고, 문헌에서 가장 많이 검증된 방식입니다(Palomares et al., 2021).

논문이 알려주지 않는 것: 0.35 / 0.40 / 0.25라는 정확한 가중치. 이것은 우리의 설계 선택이며, 검증하고 바꿀 수 있도록 적어 두었습니다. 9장을 보세요.

7일부러 쓰지 않기로 한 것

방식끌리는 이유지금 쓰지 않는 이유
인기, 팔로워, 판매측정하기 쉬움이미 보이는 사람을 다시 보여줍니다. ArtBridge는 아직 아무도 모르는 작가를 위해 있습니다.
AI로 작품 평가"똑똑해" 보임ArtBridge는 예술에 순위를 매기지 않습니다. 누구의 그림도 대신 그리지 않고, 누구의 그림도 평가하지 않습니다.
협업 필터링행동에서 학습함작은 첫 기수에는 믿을 만한 기록이 없어서 잡음만 배우게 됩니다.
언어 모델 임베딩어떤 언어의 자유 서술도 읽음불투명하고, 비용이 들고, 이 용도로 검증되지 않았습니다. 작가 본인의 선택이 먼저입니다.
자동 일대일 배정기수 전체를 최적화함점수를 동의로 착각합니다. 모든 만남은 두 작가가 직접 골라야 합니다.

언어 때문에 점수가 낮아지는 일은 없습니다. 스와힐리어, 영어, 한국어는 번역으로 다루고, 원문은 언제나 곁에 남겨 둡니다.

8보호 장치

  • 제안은 매칭이 아닙니다. 누군가를 저장하거나 프로젝트 초안을 써도 아무것도 전송되지 않습니다. 두 사람이 모두 "예"라고 할 때만 만나고, 누구든 언제든 "아니요"라고 할 수 있습니다.
  • 작품을 어디까지 변형할 수 있는지는 더 보호적인 답이 우선합니다.
  • 18세 미만 작가는 매칭되기 전에 부모나 보호자의 이름, 이메일, 동의가 필요합니다.
  • 데이터는 최소한으로. 신청서의 답만 사용합니다. 데이터베이스가 생기기 전까지 신청서는 비공개 로컬 CSV 파일에 저장합니다.

9아직 증명하지 못한 것

작가들이 말해 주기 전까지 ArtBridge가 "효과가 있다"고 주장하지 않겠습니다. 시범 운영에서는 우리의 점수를 더 단순한 두 기준, 공통 주제만 보는 방식과 조건을 갖춘 무작위 짝과 비교하고, 다음을 측정합니다.

1Tatizo ni la pande mbili

Kwenye tovuti ya manunuzi, kitabu hakiwezi kukukataa. Kwenye ArtBridge, kila "kinachopendekezwa" ni mtu mwenye matakwa yake mwenyewe. Pendekezo linalomfurahisha msanii mmoja na kumwacha mwingine bila hamu si mkutano — ni kukataliwa kwa adabu kunakosubiri kutokea.

Utafiti unaliita hili mapendekezo ya pande mbili (reciprocal recommendation): watu wanapendekezwa kwa watu, na mafanikio yanahitaji nia kutoka pande zote mbili (Pizzato et al., 2010; Palomares et al., 2021). ArtBridge imejengwa juu ya wazo hilo. Kila namba hapa chini inakokotolewa mara mbili — mara moja kutoka mtazamo wa kila msanii — kisha zinaunganishwa ili hakuna upande unaoweza kupuuzwa.

Kila kitu kinakokotolewa tu kutokana na kile ambacho wasanii wenyewe walichagua katika ombi: maswali yale yale manane ya kuunganisha, yenye majibu yale yale, kwa wasanii wa Tanzania na wa Los Angeles.

2Kanuni nne kabla ya alama yoyote

Jozi haipati alama yoyote — haionyeshwi kabisa — isipokuwa kanuni zote nne zimetimia:

  1. Wasanii wote wawili walikubali kupendekezwa. "Bado" inamaanisha hauonekani.
  2. Kila mmoja yuko katika eneo ambalo mwingine analitafuta. (Kwa waombaji wa LA, eneo la mshirika ni Tanzania.)
  3. Wote wawili walijibu orodha nne za kuunganisha — ninacholeta, ninachotafuta, mada zangu, jinsi ninavyotaka kufanya kazi. Majibu yanayokosekana hayatoi kamwe makisio ya uhakika.
  4. Wanashiriki angalau njia moja ya kufanya kazi (k.m. wote walichagua "kazi ya pamoja"). Wasanii wawili wanaotaka miradi ya aina tofauti kabisa si mkutano.

3Vipimo vitatu, na uzito kamili

Kwa jozi inayopita, tunapima mambo matatu. Kila moja ni namba kati ya 0 na 1.

Mada za pamoja — ufanano wa Jaccard uzito 0.35

Jtheme = |TA ∩ TB| ÷ |TA ∪ TB|

Mada ambazo wote walichagua, zikigawanywa kwa mada zote ambazo yeyote kati yao alichagua. Kugawanya kwa muungano kunamaanisha kuweka alama kwenye kila kisanduku hakuongezi alama ya kuunganisha (Jaccard, 1912).

Mbinu zinazokamilishana — kiwango cha kufunika uzito 0.40

CA→B = |SeekA ∩ BringB| ÷ |SeekA|

Kati ya mbinu ambazo A anatumaini kupata, sehemu ambayo B anaweza kuleta. Hiki kina mwelekeo: B anaweza kufunika matakwa yote ya A wakati A anafunika nusu tu ya ya B. Tofauti hiyo ndiyo hasa ambayo alama ya pande mbili lazima ione.

Njia za kufanya kazi — ufanano wa Jaccard uzito 0.25

Jformat = |FA ∩ FB| ÷ |FA ∪ FB|

Kazi ya pamoja, maonyesho, bidhaa, kubadilishana barua na michoro, warsha, video.

Uzito unajumlisha 1, kwa hiyo kila alama ya upande mmoja pia iko kati ya 0 na 1:

SA→B = 0.35·Jtheme + 0.40·CA→B + 0.25·Jformat

SB→A = 0.35·Jtheme + 0.40·CB→A + 0.25·Jformat

4Kuunganisha pande mbili: wastani wa kihamoni

Sasa tuna namba mbili — jinsi B anavyomfaa A, na jinsi A anavyomfaa B. Tunaziunganisha kwa wastani wa kihamoni (harmonic mean) na kuuonyesha kati ya 100:

Muunganiko = round( 100 × 2·SA→B·SB→A ÷ (SA→B + SB→A) )

Wastani wa kihamoni hubaki karibu na namba ndogo kati ya hizo mbili. Ufaafu wa upande mmoja hauwezi kujificha nyuma ya wastani:

6Kwa nini chaguo hizi

Kwa nini ukamilishano una uzito mkubwa zaidi (0.40). Katika jaribio lililodhibitiwa, Büyükboyacı na Robbett (2019) waligundua mwingiliano mkubwa chanya kati ya kuwaacha watu wachague timu zao wenyewe na kuwaacha wabobee: timu zilizojichagua zilifanya vizuri zaidi pale ujuzi wa wanachama ulipokamilishana. ArtBridge inafuata nusu zote mbili — inatuza kile kila msanii anachoweza kumletea mwenzake, na haipangii mtu mshirika kamwe; wasanii wanachagua.

Kwa nini mada bado ni muhimu (0.35). Mikono inayokamilishana inahitaji somo la pamoja la kufanyia kazi. Simba na nyati, mama na mtoto, matumaini: mada ya pamoja inawapa wageni wawili jambo la kuzungumzia kabla hawajashiriki lugha.

Kwa nini njia za kufanya kazi ni kanuni na pia uzito (0.25). Bila njia ya pamoja hakuna mradi, kwa hiyo ni kanuni. Zaidi ya njia moja ya pamoja, makubaliano zaidi hurahisisha mipango, kwa hiyo pia inahesabiwa.

Kwa nini wastani wa kihamoni. Nia ya pande zote mbili ndiyo lengo la mapendekezo ya pande mbili (Vitale et al., 2018; Palomares, 2020). Wastani wa kihamoni unaweka lengo hilo moja kwa moja, na ndio njia iliyojaribiwa zaidi katika maandiko (Palomares et al., 2021).

Kile ambacho tafiti hazitupi: uzito kamili 0.35 / 0.40 / 0.25. Hayo ni maamuzi yetu ya usanifu, yameandikwa ili yaweze kujaribiwa na kubadilishwa — tazama sehemu ya 9.
